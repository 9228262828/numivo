NUMIVO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Summary: NUMIVO is designed as an offline-first number puzzle game. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a remote backend, cloud sync, ads, or behavioral analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NUMIVO, package com.numivo.numberrush, handles information when you use the mobile game.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the current core version of NUMIVO and these informational pages.

3. Offline-First Design

NUMIVO is designed as an offline-first number puzzle game. Normal gameplay does not require an internet connection.

4. Gameplay Data

NUMIVO stores limited gameplay information locally on your device, including best scores, Classic best time, No Mistake best boards, games played, total correct taps, and best combo.

5. Classic Mode Data

The app may store your best Classic completion time and best Classic score locally on your device.

6. Time Rush Data

The app may store your best Time Rush score locally on your device.

7. No Mistake Data

The app may store your best No Mistake score and the highest number of boards cleared.

8. Games Played

The app may keep a local count of completed game sessions.

9. Correct Taps

NUMIVO may store the total number of correct number taps completed across game sessions.

10. Best Combo

NUMIVO may store your highest combo achieved during gameplay.

11. Dark Mode Preference

Your selected dark-mode preference may be stored locally so NUMIVO can remember your appearance choice.

12. Haptic Feedback Preference

Your haptic-feedback preference may be stored locally so NUMIVO can remember whether vibration feedback is enabled.

13. Local Storage

The current core version stores the gameplay information described above locally using application storage such as SharedPreferences.

14. No Account Required

You do not need to create a NUMIVO account, profile, username, or password to play the current core version.

15. No Login Data

Because the current core version has no login system, NUMIVO does not request account credentials for gameplay.

16. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, analytics, remote databases, or remote configuration.

17. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your gameplay progress to a NUMIVO-operated remote backend.

18. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide NUMIVO-operated cloud synchronization of scores or preferences.

19. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain NUMIVO-operated advertising.

20. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

21. No Cross-App Tracking

NUMIVO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

22. No Sale of Personal Information

NUMIVO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored gameplay information described in this Policy.

23. Permissions

The described core features do not require access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, photos, media, files, or payment information.

24. Location

The current core version does not require precise or approximate location information.

25. Camera

The current core version does not require camera access.

26. Photos and Media

The current core version does not require photo-library, gallery, or media-library access for normal gameplay.

27. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access, voice recording, or audio capture.

28. Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your device contacts.

29. Phone and SMS

The current core version does not require phone-call, call-log, or SMS permissions.

30. Calendar

The current core version does not require access to your calendar.

31. Files and Documents

The current core version does not require access to personal files or documents for normal gameplay.

32. Payments

The current core version does not require payment-card details, banking credentials, or payment account information.

33. Number Boards

NUMIVO generates number boards locally on your device using random values for gameplay.

34. Number Order

The app compares number taps against the current required ascending order to determine correct and incorrect selections.

35. Timers

NUMIVO uses local timers to manage Classic timing, the 60-second Time Rush mode, and internal game transitions.

36. Score Logic

Scores are calculated locally from correct taps, combos, cleared boards, and mode-specific rules.

37. Wrong Tap Logic

In supported modes, an incorrect number selection may reduce score or end the run depending on the selected game mode.

38. Combo Logic

NUMIVO may calculate a combo from consecutive correct taps. Combo values are used only for gameplay and local statistics.

39. Difficulty Progression

Board size may increase gradually as you clear more boards. This progression is calculated locally.

40. Pause and Resume

Pause and resume features operate locally and do not require network access.

41. Statistics

Statistics such as games played, correct taps, best combo, best score, best time, and boards cleared are derived from local gameplay data.

42. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data generally remains on your device until you reset progress, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

43. Reset Progress

NUMIVO includes a reset option that deletes locally stored best results and gameplay statistics while leaving the app available for continued use.

44. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling NUMIVO may remove locally stored gameplay information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

45. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by NUMIVO's core gameplay logic.

46. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include NUMIVO-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover deleted scores, timing records, statistics, or preferences.

47. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode, PIN, password, or biometric lock.

48. Children

NUMIVO is a casual number puzzle game intended for a general audience. The current core version is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, profiles, forms, messaging, or social features.

49. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process technical, account, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. Those practices are separate from NUMIVO's local gameplay data.

50. Google Play

If you obtain NUMIVO through Google Play, Google may process app-installation, account, device, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and terms.

51. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit NUMIVO gameplay data.

52. Support Communications

If you contact us at ldtd5156@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, secret keys, or other highly sensitive information.

53. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

54. International Use

You may use NUMIVO in different countries. Local gameplay data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

55. Future Features

If future releases add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, leaderboards, online multiplayer, social features, user uploads, or new permissions, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

56. Your Choices

You can choose whether to enable haptic feedback, use dark mode, reset progress, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or stop using NUMIVO.

57. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when NUMIVO changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change.

58. Contact

For privacy questions about NUMIVO, contact ldtd5156@gmail.com.
